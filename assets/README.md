# DeepCancer Research Assets

Visual assets used in the public technical documentation of the DeepCancer research project.

Assets may include experimental evaluation figures, architecture diagrams, explainability examples, and research prototype interfaces.

## Scope

The visual materials in this directory document:

- Model architecture and processing workflow
- Binary classification evaluation
- Multiclass classification evaluation
- Training behavior
- ROC analysis
- Grad-CAM explainability
- Research prototype interfaces

Experimental figures represent results obtained within the published research setting and must not be interpreted as evidence of clinical validation.

DeepCancer is a research and educational prototype and is not intended for clinical diagnosis or treatment decisions.
